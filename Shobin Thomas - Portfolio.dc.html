Behind the work

Shobin is a Dubai-based concept designer. He works across brand identity, product interfaces and generative-AI systems — sometimes one, often all three on the same project. He also shoots and directs: photography, videography, and the edit that follows, cutting video and finishing stills himself so the idea survives from frame to final grade.
